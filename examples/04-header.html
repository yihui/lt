Title and subtitle

lt_header() puts a title, and optionally a subtitle, above the table. Wrap text in I() for raw HTML.

library(lt)
d = penguins[1:3, c(1, 3, 6)]
lt(d) |> lt_header("Palmer Penguins", "First three observations")

lt(d) |> lt_header(I("<em>Palmer</em> Penguins"))
