Serving big data from R

lt_serve() keeps the rows in R and lets the browser fetch one page at a time, so million-row tables stay snappy. It needs a live R session, so print it at the console rather than knitting it.

library(lt)
big = data.frame(id = 1:1e6, x = rnorm(1e6), g = sample(letters, 1e6, TRUE))
lt_viewer(big) |> lt_serve()
