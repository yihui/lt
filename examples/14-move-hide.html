Moving and hiding columns

lt_move() reorders columns (after = NULL moves them to the front), and lt_hide() drops columns from view while their data stays in the table for anything that reads whole rows, such as an interactive row detail.

library(lt)
tbl = lt(head(iris))
tbl |> lt_move(~ Species, after = NULL)

tbl |> lt_hide(~ Petal.Length + Petal.Width)
